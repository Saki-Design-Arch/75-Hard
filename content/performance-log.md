## Weekly read

Once a week, answer four questions in writing. Guessing is worse than not looking.

1. Which post outperformed and what was its first three seconds?
2. Which post died and where did the retention curve drop?
3. What did the comments ask for that is not on the calendar yet?
4. What is the one change for next week, stated as one sentence?
