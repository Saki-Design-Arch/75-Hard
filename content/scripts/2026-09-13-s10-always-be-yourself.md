## Notes

This is the most important video of the pilot day and the easiest one to overplay. No music
swell, no gym, no shouting, no pacing. Say it like you are telling one person something
slightly embarrassing that turned out to be fine.

Convicting, not condemning. The line "you are not behind" is the whole difference.
