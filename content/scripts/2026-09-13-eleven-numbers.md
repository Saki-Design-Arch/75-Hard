## Notes

The temptation in this video is to explain the pound away with water or muscle. Do not.
Nothing measured either of those, and the moment he explains away a bad number the account
stops being worth trusting on a good one.

No shame either. The delivery is flat and factual. The video is not about the pound, it is
about tracking one number instead of eleven, and that is a mistake almost everyone
watching is making right now.
