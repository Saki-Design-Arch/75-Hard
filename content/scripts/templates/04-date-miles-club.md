## Day types and their shape

**Ordinary solo day (days 1-15 most months):** the date, the distance, the walk/run,
the streak number. 20-25 sec.

**Ordinary team day (day 16 to month end):** same, plus who else showed up - a name, a
comment, a duet. 25-30 sec.

**Ceiling day (date ≥ 3):** name that today is a ceiling day explicitly - "today's a 9th,
the date says nine, the club says three" - the rule stated matter-of-factly, not as a
complaint.

**Recovery pass day:** the pass is not a failure. Say why it's being used, plainly, no
shame.

**Milestone (15th, 16th, month close, quarter ceiling change, marathon months):** Chapter
Mark format, longer, the arc gets named.

## Notes
