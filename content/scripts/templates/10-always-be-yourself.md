## Notes

The easiest series to overplay. No music swell, no gym, no shouting, no pacing - said
like telling one person something slightly embarrassing that turned out to be fine.
Convicting, not condemning: "you already know what you're avoiding," never "you're
behind." The signature line is the point of the video, not a sign-off.
