## Notes

Never explain a number away with a mechanism nothing measured - no "it's just water,"
no "that's muscle," unless something actually measured it. Round against himself, not
in his favor (a weight rounds up, never down). A bad number gets posted the same week
as a good one; that rule is the account's whole credibility on this series.
