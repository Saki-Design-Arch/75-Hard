## Notes

Same bar, same angle, every episode - the whole series is a visual before/after that only
works if nothing else in frame changes. Film every failed attempt uncut; the failure is
what makes the eventual first rep worth anything. The series only ends one way: post the
first rep, uncut, the day it happens, and close the arc in that same video.
