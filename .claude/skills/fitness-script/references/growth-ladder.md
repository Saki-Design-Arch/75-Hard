# The ladder to a million
> **Status: parked, 2026-09-21.** Saki decided Date Miles Club stays parked (`aby/decisions/0008`). The October 1, 2026 launch below is superseded and must not appear in any script. The ceiling design stands for whenever it is unparked, and its dates get recalculated from the real start.

Growth is not the goal. Reach is the mechanism. The goal is a Date Miles Club with real
people in it and a personal training roster that is full. This file says what to post
at each stage and which ask is live, so a CTA never shows up before the account has
earned it.

Nothing in this file states a platform threshold, payout, or price as fact. Anything
marked `VERIFY` gets checked against the real source before it drives a decision, and
anything marked `CONFIRM` needs Saki's answer.

## Stage 0 · Foundation · now, under 1,000

**Job:** build the library and the habit. The account has no audience yet, so nothing
is wasted - every post is a rep.

**Post:** Proof and Process, heavy. One post a day minimum, no exceptions. Day counter
in every Process video from day one so the series is already running when people
arrive.

**CTA:** none. Zero asks. A brand new account asking for anything reads as desperate.
The only close is a forward looking line.

**Do now, not later:**
- Film every session even when nothing gets posted.
- Keep day one footage in a folder that never gets deleted.
- Bio says exactly what the account is: the weight, the standard, the timeline.
- Same posting time every day.

## Stage 1 · Signal · 1,000 to 10,000

**Job:** find which pillar the algorithm is rewarding and feed it without abandoning
the others.

**Post:** whatever is landing, plus one Honesty post a week no matter what. Start
Answer Cuts - by now there are comments, and answered comments are the cheapest trust
in existence.

**CTA:** soft follow, tied to the arc. "I'm testing the mile again in two weeks, follow
so you see it." Never "follow for more."

**New here:** reply to every comment for the first hour. Pin the comment that asks the
question the most people have.

## Stage 2 · Community · 10,000 to 100,000

**Job:** turn viewers into people who know each other. This is where the Date Miles
Club stops being an idea.

**Post:** the milestone payoffs land in here - first pull up, sub nine mile, under 250.
Add duets and stitches of people doing the same thing. Start putting other people in
the videos.

**CTA:** the list. One place to join, one line: "I'm starting a run club for people who
hate running. Link's in the bio." Collect emails and phone numbers off platform,
because an audience that only exists on TikTok is rented.

**Date Miles Club, open to people:** launched 2026-10-01 with one member, by design.
- The full charter, the arithmetic, and the launch sequence are in `date-miles-club.md`.
  Read that file before writing any club copy.
- The club launches before this stage, solo, because the charter makes days one through
  fifteen solo by design. What unlocks at 10,000 is inviting people into it.
- Team days are the ask: same miles, shared effort, in person or online. Three people is
  a team. Five is a launch.
- The pitch is the calendar, not a workout. "The date sets the distance." Anyone
  understands it in four seconds, which is why it will travel.
- Never invite people into a mileage rule that has not been checked. The month total gets
  recalculated every time the rule changes.

## Stage 3 · Offer · 100,000 to 1,000,000

**Job:** convert reach into two real businesses without changing the tone that built
it.

**Post:** the same content that got here. The mistake every account makes at this size
is switching from documenting to selling, and the tone goes first.

**CTA:** training, but earned. By this stage the results are on camera and undeniable,
which means the pitch is a fact and not a promise: "This is what I did. If you want
me to run it for you, applications are open."

**Personal training relaunch:**
- `CONFIRM` credential status with Saki before a single video mentions coaching, and
  `VERIFY` whatever certification he holds or is renewing. Until then content stays at
  "here is what I am doing."
- Launch with a waitlist, not open enrollment. Small roster, real results, filmed with
  permission.
- Price gets set from real numbers, never guessed in a script.

## The CTA ladder

Only one ask is live at a time. Moving up a rung happens when the stage changes, not
when a video does well.

| Stage | The only ask |
|---|---|
| Under 1,000 | Nothing. Close forward, not with a request |
| 1,000 to 10,000 | Follow, tied to a specific upcoming moment |
| 10,000 to 100,000 | Join the list · Date Miles Club team days |
| 100,000 plus | Training waitlist |

Comments are the exception at every stage: asking a real question and answering it is
always allowed and always works.

## What actually compounds

Reach comes from four things on this account, in order:

1. **The arc.** People follow a number that is moving. The day counter and the mile
   time do more than any hook.
2. **Honesty.** The missed day posts travel further than the PR posts, every time.
3. **Consistency.** One a day for a year is the entire strategy. The account that
   posts 300 times beats the account that posts 40 perfect times.
4. **Payoff.** Every open loop gets closed on camera. The first pull up video is worth
   more than the fifty videos of failing at it, and it only exists because those fifty
   exist.

## What burns it down

Buying followers. Faking a number. Selling before the results are on camera. Going
quiet for two weeks. Getting into a fight in the comments. Switching to whatever is
trending the moment growth slows. Any one of these costs more than a month of posting
earns.
