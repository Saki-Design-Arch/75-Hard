# Pillars, mix, and cadence

## The five pillars

**1. Proof** - the numbers moving. PRs, weigh ins, timed tests, rep counts, the mile
dropping. This pillar is what makes the account credible. Nothing else works without it.

**2. Process** - what today actually looked like. The session, the food, the 4 am
alarm, the ruck, the boring middle. This pillar is the volume of the account and the
reason someone comes back tomorrow.

**3. Honesty** - the missed workout, the bad weigh in, the day it was ugly, the thing
that hurt. The rarest pillar on fitness TikTok and the one that builds the deepest
trust. Never skipped for image management.

**4. Identity** - why a man at 258 pounds is doing this at all. Straight to lens, short,
quiet, no music swell. This is the pillar that converts a viewer into someone who
follows a person instead of watching a clip. Ends with the signature line.

**5. Teaching** - the thing he learned the hard way, said plainly, from inside the
journey. "Here is what I am doing and why," never "here is what you should do."

## Weekly mix

The training cycle lands on calendar weeks: cycle day 1 is Sunday, so the week has a
fixed shape and the content plan can key off it. Minimum one post a day. When batching
is real, two to three a day with the extras coming from Process and Teaching.

| Day | Session | Pillar | Format |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sun | Push Strength + Core | Process | Session Cut |
| Mon | Conditioning A - Engine | Teaching | Hard Lesson or Answer Cut - quiet session, good day to talk |
| Tue | Pull Strength + Posterior Chain | Proof | PR Log - this is where the pull up arc lives |
| Wed | Marine PT Circuit | Process | Session Cut - the best effort footage of the week |
| Thu | Conditioning B - Run | Proof or Process | The mile, the run progression |
| Fri | Long Ruck + Mobility | Process | Ruck Long, the one long form slot |
| Sat | Rest / Active Recovery | Identity and Honesty | Mirror Talk, then the week review. Batch day |

Never two of the same format back to back. Never two videos that open the same way in
the same week. If the week produced a real milestone, it takes the Tuesday or Thursday
slot and everything else shifts.

## Cadence rules

- One post a day is the floor. A mediocre real post beats a skipped day.
- Post between 6 and 8 am or 6 and 9 pm local, and keep one slot consistent so the
  same people keep seeing it.
- Film in batches, post daily. Saturday is the batch day - it is the rest day, so the
  time is already there.
- Every training session gets filmed even when it will not be posted. Unused footage
  is next week's B roll and it costs nothing to have.
- Reply to comments for the first hour after posting, in words, not emojis.

## Series

Ten series, each pulling from a different category of fitness TikTok, with a day one pilot
for each. The full slate, what each one is, the pilot day shoot order grouped by setup, and
the kill rule are in `content/series.md` at the repository root.

The spine is **The Standard · Day X** - the day counter climbing. It carries the account on
its own and it is the last series to ever get cut.

Ten is a pilot slate, not a schedule. Two weeks after the pilots, four survive: the Date
Miles Club plus three chosen on watch time, saves and shares. Likes decide nothing.

Hold a series until its arc closes. The audience gets paid off at the end, and that payoff
video is what crosses the account into a new tier.

## What this account does not post

Trend dances, thirst framing, transformation content stolen from other people, fake
before and afters, supplement pitches, "what I eat in a day" as a flex, anything that
requires pretending the work is easy or already finished.
