# Formats

Nine repeatable builds. Each one is a shape he can film without thinking. Timings are
targets, not laws - cut a beat before you let a video run slack.

Default framing: vertical 9:16, phone locked on a tripod or propped, face in frame for
any talking beat, eyes to the lens and not to the screen.

---

## 1. Session Cut · 20 to 35 sec · Process

The workhorse. Today's training, compressed.

| Beat | Time | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | 0 to 3 s | One line naming the day and the hard part. On screen: "Day X · Phase Y" |
| Setup | 3 to 8 s | What the session is and the one thing he is trying to beat today |
| Work | 8 to 25 s | Three to five clips, hardest set last, real audio under the voiceover |
| Number | 25 to 30 s | What it came out to, on screen as text |
| Close | 30 to 35 s | One line forward. CTA if the video earned it |

Shot list: one wide of the setup, two mid clips of working sets, one close on the face
mid effort, one clip of the last rep.

---

## 2. PR Log · 15 to 25 sec · Proof

A number moved. Shortest and highest performing format on the account.

| Beat | Time | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | 0 to 3 s | The old number, flat. On screen: the day one number, big |
| The attempt | 3 to 14 s | Unedited. One take. Do not cut inside the rep |
| The new number | 14 to 18 s | On screen next to the old one |
| Close | 18 to 25 s | What it means for the next one. No celebration speech |

Rule: the attempt clip is never sped up and never cut. The whole value is that it is
real.

---

## 3. Honest Day · 20 to 40 sec · Honesty

The one nobody else posts.

| Beat | Time | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | 0 to 3 s | Say the failure first. No wind up, no "so today was rough" |
| What happened | 3 to 15 s | Plainly. No excuse chain, no self flagellation |
| Why it happened | 15 to 25 s | The real reason, the one that is slightly embarrassing |
| What changes tomorrow | 25 to 35 s | One specific, small, verifiable thing |
| Close | 35 to 40 s | Streak stays alive because the plan survived, not because the day was perfect |

Never film this one on a day it did not happen. Never end it with a lesson that
flatters him.

---

## 4. Mirror Talk · 15 to 25 sec · Identity

Straight to lens, one idea, no B roll, no music or something quiet underneath.

| Beat | Time | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | 0 to 3 s | The sentence someone needed to hear, said to one person |
| The turn | 3 to 15 s | Where it comes from in his own week. Specific, small, true |
| Close | 15 to 25 s | The action, then "Your identity is your greatest strength. Always Be Yourself." |

The signature line is not a sign off gimmick here. It is the point of the video.

---

## 5. Hard Lesson · 25 to 40 sec · Teaching

Something learned by getting it wrong.

| Beat | Time | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | 0 to 3 s | The mistake, named |
| The cost | 3 to 12 s | What it actually cost - a week, a wrist, a month of plateau |
| The fix | 12 to 30 s | What he does now instead, exactly |
| Close | 30 to 40 s | Who this is for, in one line |

Always "here is what I am doing," never "here is what you should do." No pedestal.

---

## 6. Answer Cut · 15 to 30 sec · Teaching

Comment or DM on screen, answered to camera.

| Beat | Time | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | 0 to 2 s | Read the comment out loud, exactly as written |
| Answer | 2 to 20 s | Direct. One answer, not four caveats |
| Proof | 20 to 25 s | The clip or number that backs it |
| Close | 25 to 30 s | Invite the next question |

Answer the rude ones too, calmly, once. That video always travels.

---

## 7. Number Check · 15 to 25 sec · Proof

Weigh in or timed retest. Only on real data days.

| Beat | Time | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | 0 to 3 s | The number he expected, or the one he was afraid of |
| The reveal | 3 to 10 s | The scale or the timer, on camera, unedited |
| Context | 10 to 20 s | Against day one, not against last week. The trend is the story |
| Close | 20 to 25 s | The next target and the date he tests it |

A bad number gets posted the same day as a good one. That rule is the account's whole
credibility.

---

## 8. Chapter Mark · 30 to 60 sec · Identity and Proof

Phase changes, month marks, milestones, retests.

| Beat | Time | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | 0 to 3 s | Where it started, in one number |
| Then | 3 to 15 s | Day one footage, unflattering, unedited |
| Now | 15 to 35 s | The same test today, same framing |
| The gap | 35 to 50 s | What closed the gap, said as process and not as a secret |
| Close | 50 to 60 s | Where the next chapter goes, then the signature line |

Shoot every test with the same framing every time so these cuts are possible later.
This is why day one footage gets kept forever.

---

## 9. Ruck Long · 45 to 90 sec · Process

Saturday's loaded walk, the account's one long form slot.

| Beat | Time | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | 0 to 3 s | The weight on his back and the time he has to carry it |
| Out | 3 to 30 s | Movement, breath, real audio. Talk while walking, unpolished |
| The middle | 30 to 60 s | The honest part - where it starts to suck and what he thinks about |
| In | 60 to 80 s | Finish, numbers on screen: distance, time, load |
| Close | 80 to 90 s | One line, then the CTA. This is the slot where the Date Miles Club gets seeded |

---

## Format hygiene

- Hook on screen as text in the first frame, readable with the sound off.
- Never open with "hey guys," "so basically," a logo, or a slow pan.
- Cut every clip one beat earlier than feels natural.
- Loop the last line into the first when the format allows it.
- One idea per video. A second idea is a second video.
