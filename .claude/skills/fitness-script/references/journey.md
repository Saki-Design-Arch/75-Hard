## Daily non negotiables

Step target, ten minutes of mobility, seven to nine hours of sleep, 180 to 200 g
protein, 150 to 180 oz water. Calories 2,100 to 2,250 in phases 1 and 2, fat 70 to
80 g floor.

## The body, honestly

Left wrist does not tolerate flat palm loading, so pressing runs on fists,
parallettes, or bars. Knees get protected on running progression - rowing and biking
replace runs whenever knees, shins, or hips complain. These are not weaknesses to hide
in the content. They are the reason the plan is smart, and they are what makes it
believable to a 250 lb viewer who also has something that hurts.

## The arc the account is documenting

Day one: 256 lb, zero pull ups, an 11.8 minute mile.

Where it is going: first pull up, sub nine minute mile, sub eight, eventually a mile
under six. Loaded rucks at 25 to 35 lb. A Marine PT test standard. The weight is a
side effect of the standard, not the point.

Named milestones worth their own video, in the order they will probably arrive:

1. First unassisted pull up
2. 30 second dead hang
3. Under 250 lb
4. Sub nine minute mile
5. 60 second hollow hold
6. 25 push ups strict
7. Under 240 lb
8. First 35 lb ruck, full hour
9. Sub eight minute mile
10. First full PT test attempt

## What is off limits

No claimed Marine affiliation, service, or credential. The program is Marine style
conditioning, self coached, and that is how it always gets said.

No coaching advice pitched as certified until personal training is actually live and
credentialed. Until then the content is "here is what I am doing," never "here is what
you should do."

No medical claims. Injuries get described, never diagnosed.
