# Hooks

The first three seconds decide whether the other thirty exist. Everything in this file
is built from numbers that are actually in the tracker, which is why it works - the
hook is not a trick, it is the truth stated bluntly.

## The laws

1. **Say the number.** "I weigh 256 pounds" beats "I'm overweight" every time. Specific
   is believable, vague is scrollable.
2. **Lead with the worst fact.** The weakness is the hook. Zero pull ups is more
   interesting than any goal he could state.
3. **No wind up.** The first word is the hook. Cut "so," "okay so," "today I," "guys."
4. **On screen text in frame one.** Most viewers hear nothing for the first second.
   The text carries the hook alone.
5. **Open a loop the video has to close.** A question, a stake, a number that does not
   make sense yet.
6. **Never promise what the video does not pay off.** A hook that oversells is the
   fastest way to train the algorithm that people leave.
7. **Say it in seven words or less** where possible. Long hooks are unread hooks.

## Structures that keep working

| Structure | Shape | Example off his real numbers |
|---|---|---|
| Flat confession | "I [embarrassing fact]." | "I can't do a single pull up." |
| Number gap | "[Bad number]. [Target number]. [Timeframe]." | "11.8 minute mile. Goal is six. Six months." |
| Day counter | "Day [X] of [hard thing] at [stat]." | "Day 29 of training for Marine standard at 256 pounds." |
| Opposite expectation | "Everyone says [X]. I did [Y]." | "Everyone starts with running. I started with a 10 second dead hang." |
| Cost reveal | "[Mistake] cost me [real cost]." | "Flat palm push ups cost me a month of wrist pain." |
| Mid action | Starts inside the hardest rep, no intro | Camera opens on the failing rep, voice comes in after |
| Direct address | "If you're [specific situation], this is for you." | "If you're 250 plus and scared of the gym, watch this." |
| Receipt | The number on screen, said out loud, nothing else | Scale reading, timer, rep count |
| Unfinished sentence | Hook cuts off and the video finishes it | "The reason I'm not doing cardio first is..." |
| Contradiction | Two true things that seem to conflict | "I'm training like a Marine and I can only do 10 push ups." |

## Bank · Proof

- "Ten push ups. That was my max four weeks ago."
- "Zero pull ups on day one. Here's where the hang is now."
- "My mile was 11 minutes 48 seconds. Watch."
- "Dead hang, day one: 10 seconds. Ten. Seconds."
- "I tested every number on day one so I couldn't lie to myself later."
- "This is what a 5 pound drop actually looks like at 256."
- "The scale went the wrong way and I'm still posting it."
- "First time the bar didn't win."

## Bank · Process

- "Day 29. Push day. My wrist decides how this goes."
- "2,100 calories, 200 grams of protein, six days of training. Here's the food."
- "This is the boring part nobody films."
- "I walked 2,784 steps a day before this started. Baseline."
- "Rest day. This is the workout most people skip."
- "45 minutes with weight on my back. Talk to me while I do it."
- "Nobody sees the ten minutes of mobility. That's the part keeping me in this."
- "Six training days, one rest day, no negotiating."

## Bank · Honesty

- "I missed a workout. Here's exactly why."
- "I ate like an idiot last night and the plan still stands."
- "This is week five and I wanted to quit on Tuesday."
- "I'm not going to pretend that session went well."
- "My knees told me to stop and I listened. Say what you want."
- "Three weeks in and I look basically the same. Still going."
- "The honest version of today: I did the minimum."
- "I hid this photo for a week. Posting it now."

## Bank · Identity

- "I stopped waiting to feel ready. That was the whole change."
- "Nobody is coming. That's not sad, that's freedom."
- "I'm 256 pounds and I'm not embarrassed. I'm early."
- "You're not lazy. You've just never been given a standard."
- "The version of me that quits has been in charge long enough."
- "I'm not building a body. I'm building someone who finishes things."
- "You can start at the bottom of the list. You cannot start at the sidelines."
- "Discipline isn't punishment. It's how I keep a promise to myself."

## Bank · Teaching

- "Flat palm push ups were wrecking my wrist. Here's what I do now."
- "I don't run when my knees complain. I row. Here's why that isn't quitting."
- "Negatives built more pull strength for me than any machine did."
- "You don't need a gym for this. You need a bar and 45 minutes."
- "Here's how I test a max without hurting myself."
- "I recalculate my calories every 15 pounds. Here's the math."
- "Dead hangs before pull ups. Every time. Here's the order."
- "The reason my first phase had almost no running in it."

## Hooks to never use again

"POV," "watch till the end," "you won't believe," "this changed my life," "let me put
you on," "nobody's talking about this," anything starting with a countdown, anything
that begs.

## When he is stuck

Ask one question: what was the hardest part of today. The answer, said flat with no
setup, is the hook. That is true almost every time.
